📊 Points Awarded

Scoring for each match:

  • 1 point for each team's goal total correctly predicted
  • 3 points for predicting the correct result (win, loss or draw)
  • 4 points for correct result + one exact team score
  • 6 points for a perfect score prediction (both scores + result correct)

Examples (Actual result: 3-1):

  • Prediction: 3-1 = 6 points (perfect prediction)
  • Prediction: 3-0 = 4 points (home score ✓ + correct result)
  • Prediction: 2-1 = 4 points (away score ✓ + correct result)
  • Prediction: 3-2 = 4 points (home score ✓ + correct result)
  • Prediction: 1-0 = 3 points (correct result only)
  • Prediction: 0-1 = 1 point (away score ✓)
  • Prediction: 1-3 = 0 points (all wrong)

🎖️ Tournament Winner Bonus

  • If the team predicted to win the tournament is the losing finalist: 5 bonus points
  • If that team wins the tournament: 12 bonus points

🔗 Tie-Breakers

When more than one person is level on points, places in the league table will be determined as follows in the order shown:

  1. The person having gained the greatest number of perfect predictions (6pt games) will be placed highest
  2. The person having gained the greatest number of correct results (3pt games) will be placed highest
